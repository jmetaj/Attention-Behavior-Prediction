# Pilot Experiment: User Tasks

## General Instructions for Participants

- Behave naturally while using the website.
- Use the mouse as you normally would.
- Do not deliberately move the mouse in a particular pattern.
- You are free to choose products and navigation paths unless the task specifies otherwise.
- There are no right or wrong product choices.
- Ask the researcher if a task is unclear.
- Complete each task before moving to the next one.

---

## Task 1

- **Task ID:** T01
- **Task title:** Initial exploration
- **Participant instruction:** “Spend approximately 1–2 minutes exploring the website. Become familiar with the available product categories and products. Do not try to complete a purchase.”
- **Objective:** Collect initial exploratory interaction behavior.
- **Expected interaction types:** Category and product browsing, page navigation, product-detail inspection, scrolling, hovering, clicking, and mouse movement.

## Task 2

- **Task ID:** T02
- **Task title:** Gaming laptop
- **Participant instruction:** “Imagine that you are looking for a laptop suitable for gaming. Find a laptop that you would consider suitable and inspect its product details.”
- **Objective:** Collect goal-directed product search and product inspection behavior.
- **Expected interaction types:** Laptop-category browsing, product-list exploration, product-detail inspection, scrolling, hovering, clicking, and navigation between pages.

## Task 3

- **Task ID:** T03
- **Task title:** GPU
- **Participant instruction:** “Imagine that you are planning to upgrade a desktop computer. Find a graphics card (GPU) that you would consider suitable and inspect its details.”
- **Objective:** Collect category-specific search and product inspection behavior.
- **Expected interaction types:** GPU-category browsing, product-list exploration, product-detail inspection, scrolling, hovering, clicking, and navigation between pages.

## Task 4

- **Task ID:** T04
- **Task title:** Mouse and keyboard
- **Participant instruction:** “Imagine that you need a mouse and a keyboard for your usual computer use. Explore the available products and identify one mouse and one keyboard that you would consider choosing.”
- **Objective:** Collect navigation across multiple product categories and product-selection behavior.
- **Expected interaction types:** Navigation between mouse and keyboard categories, product-list browsing, product-detail inspection, scrolling, hovering, clicking, and transitions between pages.

## Task 5

- **Task ID:** T05
- **Task title:** Compare laptops
- **Participant instruction:** «Φαντάσου ότι ενδιαφέρεσαι να αγοράσεις έναν φορητό υπολογιστή. Βρες δύο διαφορετικά laptop που θα εξέταζες ως πιθανές επιλογές. Άνοιξε τις πληροφορίες των δύο προϊόντων και σύγκρινέ τα. Όταν ολοκληρώσεις τη σύγκριση, πάτησε Ολοκλήρωση εργασίας και επίλεξε ποιο από τα δύο laptop θα προτιμούσες.»
- **Objective:** Collect repeated product visits, back navigation, comparison behavior, and transitions between products.
- **Expected interaction types:** Laptop-list browsing, visits to multiple product pages, repeated page visits, back navigation, scrolling, hovering, clicking, and transitions between products.

## Task 6

- **Task ID:** T06
- **Task title:** Free exploration
- **Participant instruction:** “Explore the website freely for approximately 2–3 minutes, as if you were genuinely interested in purchasing an electronic product. Browse any categories or products you find interesting and stop when you feel finished.”
- **Objective:** Collect unconstrained, self-directed browsing behavior.
- **Expected interaction types:** Participant-selected category and product browsing, navigation, product-detail inspection, scrolling, hovering, clicking, and mouse movement.
